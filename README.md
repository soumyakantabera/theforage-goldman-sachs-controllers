# Goldman Sachs PWM Operations Virtual Experience

**Role Simulated:** Operations Analyst, Private Wealth Management  
**Program:** Two-Part Virtual Experience (April 2026)  
**Focus:** Transaction facilitation, operational efficiency, risk management, and client service in a global financial institution

---

## Task 1: Personal Transaction Plan

### What You'll Learn
- Key concepts of wealth management operational support, transaction facilitation, and client service in a global financial institution
- Practical applications for operational efficiency in everyday situations such as managing personal transactions, understanding banking processes, business enablement and process improvements (including an overview of key technology such as BI tooling), and ensuring timely payments
- The structure and function of operations at Goldman Sachs and the breadth and depth of opportunities available at Goldman Sachs

### What You'll Do
- Explore and learn about the role of an analyst in operations at Goldman Sachs
- Complete a reflective exercise to practice applying key operational management skills to daily life, such as managing personal transactions or planning a large purchase and making process improvements where necessary

### Scenario & Deliverable
You plan a significant personal purchase (MacBook Pro 14" M4 Pro for professional financial modeling and client work, total $1,799 with AppleCare+ and accessories). You complete the full **Personal Transaction Plan** template covering transaction details, expense tracking, budgeting, banking reviews, transaction monitoring, process improvements, timely payments setup, and a reflective summary explicitly linked to GS PWM operations practices.

**Input:** `Task1_Personal_Transaction_Plan/Input/Personal_Transaction_Plan_Template.xlsx` (9-sheet professional template)

**Output:** `Task1_Personal_Transaction_Plan/Output/Personal_Transaction_Plan_Completed.xlsx`  
- 3 income sources ($6,035 monthly net)  
- 8 expense categories with formulas ($4,931.50 total)  
- Cross-sheet budget net position ($1,635)  
- Banking reconciliation (Chase Private Client accounts, 100% accuracy on 47 transactions)  
- Custom transaction alerts and zero discrepancies  
- Two business enablement improvements (Plaid automation + quarterly subscription audit saving $420/year)  
- 4 automated payments with calendar reminders  
- Professional formatting and reflective summary mapping personal ops to institutional WM controls

## Task 2: Trade Fail Report

### What You'll Learn
- Understand the roles of different business lines and divisions (e.g., Trading and Settlement, Asset Transfer and Authorization, Compliance) in the transaction process and their impact on operational efficiency and client satisfaction
- How to identify and analyze common issues in the monitoring, authentication, approval, and settlement of incoming and outgoing trades and asset transfers
- How to incorporate best practices in operational risk management and understand regulatory implications relevant to transactions

### What You'll Do
- Analyze financial data in a report to spot issues that might delay access to funds for a time-sensitive purchase
- Identify which divisions or roles (e.g., Trading Desk, IT Department) to work with to resolve trade issues
- Identify potential risks in ultra-high net worth transactions and consult with a senior or knowledgeable person for guidance

### Scenario & Deliverable
A UHNW client (Client A) is acquiring a Takashi Murakami painting — a time-sensitive, high-value purchase. The **Financial Data Report** shows multiple pending transactions with blockers (funds not available, compliance review, awaiting documentation, technical issues). You triage the issues, engage the right internal teams, assess risks, and produce a board-ready **Trade Fail Report**.

**Inputs:**
- `Task2_Trade_Fail_Report/Input/Financial_Data_Report.xlsx` (10 transactions, TXN001–TXN010, with status notes highlighting delays)
- `Task2_Trade_Fail_Report/Input/Trade_Fail_Report_Template.docx` (7-section corporate template)

**Output:** `Task2_Trade_Fail_Report/Output/Trade_Fail_Report_Completed.docx`  
- 3 prioritized issues (TXN008 liquidity gap, multiple compliance/approval holds on $5M+ buys, TXN010 $2.2M documentation block)  
- Actions taken (Treasury bridge financing, expedited art KYC/provenance dossier, Settlements T+0 override)  
- Teams involved (Treasury + Trading Desk | Compliance + Legal | Settlements + Client Advisory)  
- Risk assessment (High: deal failure/reputational damage; Medium: liquidity/audit exposure)  
- Mitigation strategies with SLAs (4-hour doc confirmation, daily 8 AM liquidity calls, 48-hour Firm Risk escalation)  
- Senior colleague guidance (Elena Voss, SVP PWM Operations — CRM logging + post-resolution portfolio review)  
- Strategic recommendation (new "Time-Sensitive Alternative Asset Transaction Protocol" with 72h forecasting and fast-track lanes)
